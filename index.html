About

I am a biochemistry graduate and healthcare AI engineer focused on building reliable data and machine learning systems for African health contexts. My scientific training informs how I approach evidence, evaluate methods, and interpret results in health data work.

My work bridges epidemiology, clinical data engineering, statistical modelling, and applied AI. I build systems that turn health data into usable insights, automate research workflows, and make machine learning solutions more reproducible and easier to evaluate.

I am particularly interested in public health intelligence, clinical NLP, health data infrastructure, and responsible AI for resource-constrained healthcare settings.

My approach: Precision in the data. Rigour in the methods. Impact in the real world.
